## ✨ Features

- **🏢 Department of Happiness Hero**: Glassmorphism presentation with living floating Dudu & Bubu cuddle illustration and celebratory particle bursts.
- **🗂️ Dudu Dossier**: Interactive 3D tilt employee ID badge with official access pass and *APPROVED BY BUBU™* stamp.
- **🔍 Why Dudu?**: A very serious investigation conducted by the Department of Happiness across 6 exhibits.
- **🤭 A Few Things Bubu Has to Admit…**: Progressive scroll-triggered confessions with intimate Dudu-Bubu artwork.
- **📸 My Favourite Pictures of Dudu**: Floating Pinterest polaroid lookbook with handwritten notes, heart favorites, and a full-size lightbox.
- **📊 Dudu Happiness Report**: SaaS-meets-cute dashboard metrics with real-time audit recalculator.
- **❤️ 6 Months of Us**: Interactive unfolding 3D glass envelope letter honoring the September 8, 2026 milestone.
- **🌻 The Final Reels Punchline**: *“You're not my boyfriend, but I'll still share ‘you're my saiyaan’ reels with you... Because I like you.”* with the *Usual Bubu behaviour™* seal.
- **🎵 Ambient Melodic Audio**: Lightweight Web Audio synthesizer playing gentle, peaceful piano chimes (zero MP3 dependencies).
- **📱 Mobile-First Responsive**: 60fps touch performance, safe viewports, 44px+ touch targets, and reduced-motion accessibility.
